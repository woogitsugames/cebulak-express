### Pełny opis

~~~text
Cebulak Express: Do Polski is a Polish-language arcade game about a journey familiar to many Poles living abroad. The boot is full, suitcases are stacked on the roof, and home is still hundreds of kilometres away. This time, your estate car can fly!

ONE TAP, ONE JOURNEY
Tap the screen to jump. Dodge bridges, barriers, road signs and roadworks. One mistake ends your journey, but the next attempt is only a tap away.

JARS AND PARCELS FROM HOME
Pick up collectibles along the way to earn extra kilometres. Watch out: your loaded car becomes heavier for a while. Take the risk or play it safe? You decide.

EIGHT COUNTRIES, ONE DESTINATION
Set off from Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Switzerland, Norway or Denmark. Choose your route and watch the scenery change as you travel towards Poland.

POLONIA LEAGUE
Every verified run adds kilometres to your chosen country's total. Check the leaderboard and cheer on your team.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Try the daily route under the same conditions as other players. Compare your score and give it another go.

PLAY AND SHARE
Your score is measured in kilometres. After each run, share how far you made it with friends.

No personal details to type in and no personalised ads. The leaderboard uses an anonymous identifier and a nickname selected from a predefined list.

The game itself is in Polish.

Destination: Poland. How far can you fly?
~~~
